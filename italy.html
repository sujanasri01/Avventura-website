Benvenuti in Italia 🇮🇹

Explore the timeless beauty, culture and charm of Italy.

From romantic coastlines to historic cities, discover unforgettable Italian destinations.

❤️ Amalfi Coast

Famous for: Lemon groves, dramatic coastline, romantic villages

📍 What to Explore

Positano, Amalfi Cathedral, Ravello and Fiordo di Furore.

🛍️ Shopping

Handmade ceramics, linen clothes and lemon products.

✨ Special Activity

Lemon-grove tour, cooking class, boat ride and Path of the Gods.

💕 Venice

Famous for: Canals, romance and Venetian architecture

📍 What to Explore

St. Mark's Square, Rialto Bridge and Bridge of Sighs.

🛍️ Shopping

Murano glass, Venetian masks and handmade crafts.

✨ Special Activity

Gondola ride, mask-making workshop and pasta cooking class.

🎨 Florence

Famous for: Renaissance art and culture

📍 What to Explore

Duomo, Uffizi Gallery, Ponte Vecchio and Palazzo Vecchio.

🛍️ Shopping

Leather goods, jewellery and Florentine crafts.

✨ Special Activity

Pasta-making, gelato-making class and leather workshop.

🏛️ Rome

Famous for: Ancient history and architecture

📍 What to Explore

Colosseum, Trevi Fountain, Pantheon, Roman Forum and Vatican City.

🛍️ Shopping

Italian fashion, leather products and souvenirs.

✨ Special Activity

Roman pasta-making class, Vespa tour and food tour.

👗 Milan

Famous for: Fashion, luxury and design

📍 What to Explore

Duomo, Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, Brera and Sforza Castle.

🛍️ Shopping

Designer fashion, luxury goods and Italian jewellery.

✨ Special Activity

Fashion tour, design experience and Italian cooking class.

🍕 Naples

Famous for: Pizza and Neapolitan culture

📍 What to Explore

Historic centre, Naples Cathedral and Castel dell'Ovo.

🛍️ Shopping

Handmade crafts and traditional food products.

✨ Special Activity

Authentic pizza-making class and street-food tour.

🍝 Bologna

Famous for: Fresh pasta and food culture

📍 What to Explore

Piazza Maggiore, Two Towers and historic porticoes.

🛍️ Shopping

Traditional food products and local markets.

✨ Special Activity

Fresh pasta-making class.

🌊 Cinque Terre

Famous for: Colorful coastal villages

📍 What to Explore

Monterosso, Vernazza, Corniglia, Manarola and Riomaggiore.

🛍️ Shopping

Local crafts and handmade souvenirs.

✨ Special Activity

Hiking, boat trip and pesto-making class.

🌋 Sicily

Famous for: Ancient culture, beaches and distinctive cuisine

📍 What to Explore

Palermo, Taormina, Mount Etna and Syracuse.

🛍️ Shopping

Ceramics, traditional Sicilian crafts and local food.

✨ Special Activity

Cannoli-making, street-food tour and Etna excursion.

💎 Capri

Famous for: Glamour, sea caves and luxury

📍 What to Explore

Blue Grotto, Faraglioni and Anacapri.

🛍️ Shopping

Handmade sandals, perfumes, linen and designer goods.

✨ Special Activity

Boat tour, snorkeling and scenic hiking.

✈️ Basic Travel Information

A few things to know before exploring Italy

🗣️

Language

Italian is the main language. English is commonly understood in tourist areas.

💶

Currency

Italy uses the Euro (€). Cards are widely accepted.

☀️

Best Time

Spring and autumn are popular for comfortable sightseeing.

🚆

Transportation

Trains, buses, boats and local public transport connect major destinations.

📱

Travel Essentials

Carry your passport, travel documents, phone and suitable walking shoes.
